Step 1

Open the ECAE Website or Portal

  1. Open the Emirates College for Advanced Education (ECAE) website.
  2. Click Login in the top-right corner.
  3. Select ECAE Portal.
  4. You can also open the portal directly using the links below.
Step 2

Sign In and Select Students

  1. Sign in using your ECAE username and password.
  2. After signing in, select the Students tab.
If you cannot access the portal, reset your password through Office 365 and try again. Important:
Students using other corporate devices may need to sign in using the following username format:

ECAE\StudentID
Example: ECAE\001164
Step 3

Locate Feedback Follow-Up

  1. After selecting the Students tab, look at the right-side panel.
  2. Find the Important Links (Internal) section.
  3. Select Feedback Follow-Up.
Feedback Follow-Up is available from the Important Links (Internal) section.
Step 4

Check Feedback Status

  1. Open the Feedback Follow-Up service.
  2. Enter the required information.
  3. Search for your previously submitted feedback or complaint.
  4. Review the current status and updates.
This service allows students to check the status of a previously submitted feedback or complaint.
