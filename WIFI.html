Device Selection

Which Device Are You Using?

  1. Select the type of device you want to connect.
  2. You will only see the instructions for your selected device.
The Wi-Fi settings are different for IOS and Android devices.
iOS Step 1

Open Wi-Fi Settings

  1. Open Settings on your IOS.
  2. Select Wi-Fi.
  3. Select ECAE-BYOD from the available networks.
iOS Step 2

Enter Your ECAE Account Details

  1. Enter your ECAE username.
  2. Enter your ECAE account password.
  3. Tap Join or Connect.
Enter your Student ID as the username.

Example: 002091
iOS Step 3

Trust the Connection

  1. Wait for the certificate information to appear.
  2. Tap Trust to complete the setup.
  3. Wait for your device to connect to ECAE-BYOD.
Your IOS device should now be connected to ECAE-BYOD.
Android Step 1

Open Wi-Fi Settings

  1. Open Wi-Fi settings on your Android device.
  2. Select ECAE-BYOD from the available networks.
Android Step 2

Configure the Wi-Fi Settings

  1. Select PEAP as the EAP method.
  2. Select MSCHAPV2 for Phase 2 authentication.
  3. Set the certificate option to Don't validate.
  4. Enter your ECAE Student ID in the Identity field.
  5. Leave the Anonymous Identity field blank.
  6. Enter your ECAE account password.
Enter your Student ID in the Identity field.

Example: 0020911
Android Step 3

Connect to ECAE-BYOD

  1. Review the connection settings.
  2. Tap Connect.
  3. Wait for your device to join the ECAE-BYOD network.
Your Android device should now be connected to ECAE-BYOD.
