Interactive Absence Support Documents Guide

Follow the instructions one step at a time.

Step 1 of 5 20%
Step 1

Open the ECAE Website or Portal

  1. Open the Emirates College for Advanced Education official website.
  2. Select Login in the top-right corner of the page.
  3. Choose ECAE Portal from the dropdown menu.
  4. You can also open the ECAE Portal directly using the button below.
Step 2

Sign In and Choose Students

  1. Sign in using your ECAE username and password.
  2. After signing in, select the Students tab.
If you cannot access the portal, reset your password through Office 365 and try again. Important:
Students using other corporate devices may need to sign in using the following username format:

ECAE\StudentID
Example: ECAE\001164
Step 3

Open Student Service

  1. On the ECAE Portal, find the Important Links (Internal) section.
  2. This section is located on the right-side panel of the ECAE Portal.
  3. Select Student Service.
Step 4

Select Absence Support Documents

  1. On the Student Service homepage, find the available student services.
  2. Select Absence Support Documents.
  3. You can also use the direct Student Service link below.
The Absence Support Documents service allows students to provide supporting documents related to approved absences.
Step 5

Complete the Service

  1. Open Absence Support Documents.
  2. Fill in the requested information and documents.
Make sure your supporting document is clear and that the information you provide is correct.
