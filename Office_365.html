Interactive Office 365 Guide

Follow the instructions one step at a time.

Step 1 of 4 25%
Step 1

Open the ECAE Portal

  1. Open the ECAE Portal using the button below.
  2. Wait for the ECAE Portal sign-in page to open.
Step 2

Sign In to the ECAE Portal

  1. Enter your ECAE username.
  2. Enter your ECAE password.
  3. Select Sign In.
  4. After signing in, select the Students tab.
Important:

Students using other corporate devices may need to use the following username format:

ECAE\StudentID
Example: ECAE\001164
Step 3

Open ECAE Office 365

  1. Look at the right-side panel of the ECAE Portal.
  2. Find the Important Links (Internal) section.
  3. Select ECAE Office 365.
Select the ECAE Office 365 link shown in the screenshot.
Step 4

Sign In to Office 365

  1. Enter your ECAE email address.
  2. Select Next.
  3. Enter your password.
  4. Select Sign In.
  5. You can now access available Microsoft 365 services and applications.
You can also open Microsoft 365 directly using the button below.
Step 1

Sign In to Microsoft 365

  1. Open Microsoft 365 using the button below.
  2. Select Sign In.
  3. Enter your ECAE email address.
  4. Select Next.
  5. Enter your password and complete the sign-in process.
Step 2

Open the Download Apps Menu

  1. After signing in, look at the top-right corner of the page.
  2. Select the three-dot menu (...).
  3. Select Download apps.
The screenshot highlights both the three-dot menu and the Download apps option.
Step 3

Select Your Device

  1. Wait for the application download page to open.
  2. Select Download for Windows if you are using a Windows laptop or desktop.
  3. Select Download for mobile if you are using a phone or tablet.
Choose the option that matches the device you are currently using.
Step 4

Open the Downloaded File

  1. Wait for the application file to finish downloading.
  2. Open the downloaded file from your browser's Downloads section.
  3. If your device asks for permission, review the message and select the appropriate option to continue.
  4. Follow the instructions shown by the installer.
  5. After installation, open the application and sign in with your ECAE account if requested.
The appearance and name of the downloaded file may differ depending on the device and browser being used.
